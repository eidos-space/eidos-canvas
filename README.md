# Canvas

Arrange notes, links, and files on a canvas in Eidos Lite. Canvas opens and saves the `.canvas` files used by Obsidian.

## Open your first canvas

1. In Eidos Lite, open **Plugins → Marketplace**, find **Canvas**, and install it. Enable Canvas in the Space where you want to use it.
2. Choose **New File → Canvas (.canvas)** to start a blank board. To open an existing `.canvas` file, right-click it in the Space Explorer and choose **Open with → Canvas**.

## Build your board

- **Add cards and groups.** Pick a tool from the bottom bar or press **I** to open the insert menu. Press **T**, **F**, **L**, or **G** for text, file, link, or group. Click to place a card and edit it in place. Drag to draw a group.
- **Arrange and connect.** Drag a card to move it. Select it and drag a corner to resize it, or choose **Color** for a preset. To connect two cards, select the first, press **C**, then click the second. Double-click a card or connection to edit it.
- **Navigate and save.** Drag the background to pan and scroll over it to zoom. Use **+** and **−** to zoom, or **0** to fit the whole board. Press **⌘S** on macOS or **Ctrl+S** on Windows and Linux to save.

## Use Markdown from your Space

Drag a Markdown file from the Space Explorer onto the board. The card previews the original and refreshes after you save changes to that file. In Eidos Lite 0.38.0 or later, click the card's title to open the original in the right sidebar while keeping the canvas visible. Earlier versions of Lite open the file in the main editor.

If you move or rename a Markdown file, drop it onto its unavailable card to restore the reference and keep the card's position and connections. You can also choose **Relink file** and enter its new path within the Space. When moving a board to another Space, bring along its referenced files so the previews can still find them.
